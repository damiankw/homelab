Rust 2024: The Road to Faster Compilation

John Doe
Oct 24, 2023

The primary complaint against Rust has always been its compilation speed. But that's about to change.

Rust's compiler does a lot for you. It checks every reference, validates every lifetime, and ensures that your concurrent code is free of data races. This work is computationally expensive. As projects grow from thousands to millions of lines of code, these compile times can become a significant bottleneck in developer productivity.

Parallel Front-end

One of the most anticipated features in the 2024 edition is the parallelization of the compiler front-end. Historically, the analysis phases of `rustc` have been mostly single-threaded. By leveraging the same concurrency principles that Rust promotes, the compiler team has managed to distribute this workload across multiple cores.

Internal Benchmark:

Rust 2021: 180s
Rust 2024: 72s

The reduction in feedback loops for developers will be transformative. It's not just about the final build; it's about the speed of `cargo check` and the responsiveness of the Language Server (LSP) while writing code.
